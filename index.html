About Us

Three Cities. One Spirit.

Our story began with a simple belief: polo is more than a sport — it is a way of life. A world where heritage, freedom, travel, beauty, and attention to detail come together.

That vision led us to three different places around the world — three destinations with three distinct personalities, yet all connected by the same spirit.

In Santa Barbara, polo meets sunshine, nature, ease, and the freedom of the American West Coast. This is where a world begins in which clothing, bags, eyewear, and accessories are refined, yet effortless and made for real life. Santa Barbara Polo & Racquet Club represents the bright, sporty, and relaxed side of our world.

From there, the journey continues to Italy — to San Marco. Here, European elegance, classical architecture, and the art of dressing well become part of the story. Polo San Marco is the more sophisticated and urban expression of the family: navy and gold, clean lines, and a sense of quiet confidence that never needs to shout to be noticed.

But the story would not be complete without Venice.

Venezia Polo is inspired by a city that has always lived somewhere between reality and dream — water, stone, light, art, and centuries of history. Here, polo becomes more daring, more artistic, and more romantic. Deep colors, unexpected details, and inspiration drawn from Venice’s canals, palaces, and hidden streets shape the character of Venezia Polo.

To us, these are not simply three separate brands. They are three chapters of one story.
