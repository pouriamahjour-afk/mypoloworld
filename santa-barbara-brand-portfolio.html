Brand Portfolio · Positioning Overview

Santa Barbara Polo & Racquet Club

A focused view of the brand's intended market space: polo heritage, California lifestyle, refined casual design and accessible-premium positioning.

01 · Brand Essence

Four ideas define the brand.

The identity is strongest when sport is treated as heritage rather than performance: polo provides the codes, while the product expression remains refined, wearable and lifestyle-led.

Polo Heritage

Equestrian codes, club culture and the visual language of polo form the historic foundation.

California Spirit

Relaxed confidence, sunlight, travel and an effortless premium-casual attitude.

Refined Casual

Clean, polished products designed for city, travel, leisure and social settings.

Accessible Premium

A more elevated feel than value-led mass fashion, while remaining approachable versus high-premium icons.

Not a universal global rankingSanta Barbara operates through regional licensing, so product ranges and pricing vary by market.

Positioning Statement
Heritage of Polo. Spirit of California. A Modern Lifestyle.

Santa Barbara Polo & Racquet Club is best presented as an accessible-premium polo lifestyle brand: more selective and lifestyle-oriented than broad value-led polo labels, while remaining approachable relative to established global premium icons.
